# HEADLINE_NARRATIVE.md — the scroll story (V5 copy fix)

Karan's finding (2026-07-30): headlines are huge and carry the whole message,
but read in sequence they don't form a coherent story — the reader gets lost,
especially where the page switches from the website offer to the software
offer with no signal.

## Diagnosis of the current sequence

1. Your new website is already built. Let's make it yours.   ✅ strong opening
2. Turn a first look into a clear next step.                 ❌ abstract — doesn't say what the section is
3. Three small steps from preview to your domain.            ✅ clear
4. Your preview is reserved for you — for seven days.        ✅ clear
5. Turn the bottleneck in your day into a working tool.      ❌ jumps to software with no topic signal; repeats the "Turn X into Y" shape of #2
6. See what owners like you received.                        ❌ website proof, but placed AFTER the software detour
7. People who trusted this work.                             ⚠️ vague ownership
8. One clear project fee for your website.                   ⚠️ arrives after software → "fee for which offer?"
9. Know exactly what happens next.                           ❌ sounds like process; it is actually the FAQ
10. Your website is waiting. Make it yours.                  ✅ good close
11. Choose the result your business needs next.              ❌ vague footer line

Root causes: (a) two offers interleaved with no boundary, (b) abstract
headlines that don't name their section, (c) proof and pricing separated from
the story they belong to, (d) repeated sentence construction.

## Approved new sequence — one continuous story

WEBSITE STORY (offer 1: 48 hours)
1. Your new website is already built. Let's make it yours.
2. Here is what it does for your business.
3. Three small steps from preview to your own domain.
4. It's yours for seven days. Then it expires.
5. See the sites other owners claimed.
6. People who trust me with their work.   (wording confirmed by Karan, present tense)
7. One clear fee. No monthly subscription.

BRIDGE → SOFTWARE STORY (offer 2: 21 days)
8. Need more than a website? I build software too.
9. Your daily bottleneck, working software in 21 days.

CLOSE
10. Questions owners ask before saying yes.
11. Your website is waiting. Make it yours.
Footer line: Tell me what you need next.

## Rules for all future headlines (permanent)

- Each headline must name its own section in plain words — a reader who reads
  ONLY the headlines must understand the whole page.
- Never switch offer/topic without an explicit bridge headline.
- No two consecutive headlines share a sentence construction.
- Website story completes (proof + price) before software begins.
- Keep the honest expiry statement in a headline, not buried in body text.
